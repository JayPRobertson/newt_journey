As the sun recedes over the horizon, the forest begins to grow dark. The Newt quivers in fear. Trees reach out their ghastly limbs, tearing at its cloak. Footsteps echo all around and eyes peer out from the darkness, watching and waiting. The Newt begins to run.
After a few minutes, the exhausted Newt stops to breathe. A glow catches its eye from between two trees. Curious, the Newt peers through the brush and sees a welcome sight: a campfire! Two critters sit on logs around the fire and invite the Newt to join them.
