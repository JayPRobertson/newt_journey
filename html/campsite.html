As the sun recedes over the horizon, the forest begins to grow dark eerie. Trees reach their ghastly limbs towards you, tearing at your clothes. Footsteps echo all around you and it feels like hidden eyes watch your every move. You begin to run, terrified.
When it seems like your legs are about to give up on you, a glow catches your eyes from between two trees. Curious, you burst through the brush. As you emerge into the clearing you see a welcome sight: a campfire! The two critters who sit around it quickly invite you to join them.

DEVELOPER NOTE: You share a campsite for a night with a bard who will sing for you and a writer who tells you tales. Host for music and writing
